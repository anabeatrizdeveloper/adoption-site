Option B

Home Delivery

If you are unable to travel, we can arrange safe transportation for your pet to your nearest major airport through a flight volunteer or professional courier.

Inquire About This Option

What it is

We arrange for your pet to be transported to your nearest major airport.

How it works

  • Via a flight volunteer, when available
  • Or through a professional animal courier

Cost

  • Adopter covers the transport cost
  • Plus standard adoption preparation fees

Preparation Included

  • Rabies titre testing
  • Vaccinations and microchipping
  • IATA-approved travel crate
  • Official export documentation

Travel Planning

Our team helps coordinate the safest and most suitable transport option based on availability, destination, and timing.

Airport Delivery

The pet is delivered to a major airport that is practical for your route and adoption arrangements.

Why choose this option?

It is ideal for adopters who cannot travel but still want a safe, guided, and structured international adoption process.

Who it is ideal for

Adopters with limited travel flexibility who prefer to receive their pet through a coordinated international delivery option.

Support Provided

We assist with preparation, documentation, and process guidance so the journey is as smooth and responsible as possible.
