About KİMCANKO

Trusted rescue work since 2018

KİMCANKO is a registered volunteer-run NGO in Türkiye focused on careful, small-scale international rescue partnerships.

Registered NGO Volunteer-run 3,100+ sterilizations EU-compliant

Medical Protocol

Prepared before travel

  • Microchip and passport
  • Parasite treatment
  • Rabies vaccination
  • Rabies titre test
  • Travel documentation
  • 90-day care and monitoring

Costs

Direct-cost model

No adoption fees are charged. Costs are estimated per rescue in advance.

Required

Microchip, passport, rabies, titre test, health certificate, carrier, transport.

Optional

Core vaccines, airport transport, route-specific support.

Timeline

90-day waiting period

After the rabies titre blood draw, travel can only happen after the mandatory 90-day waiting period is complete.

1. Match & Titre 2. 90-Day Wait 3. Final Vet Prep 4. Handover

Logistics

Transport Options

We offer two international transport pathways depending on your location, travel flexibility, and preferred adoption experience.

Structured international delivery coordinated through flight volunteers or professional pet transport services.

  • Airport handover coordination
  • Travel crate + export preparation
  • Full veterinary and documentation compliance
  • Suitable for adopters unable to travel

An immersive handover experience where an NGO representative or adopter travels to our base to meet the rescue and complete the journey together.

  • Up to 7 days accommodation in our NGO guest room in Taşucu
  • Airport pickup guidance and local support provided
  • Travel crate and export preparation included
  • Full veterinary and documentation compliance
  • Located 1.5 hours from Çukurova International Airport (COV)
  • Designed for a gradual, hands-on transition and bonding period
  • Ideal for adopters seeking a more personal and engaged experience

About Taşucu

  • Coastal base of our NGO located on the Mediterranean coast of Türkiye
  • A calm, small-town coastal environment supporting low-stress transitions for rescued animals
  • Known for its beaches, coastal scenery, and relaxed atmosphere
  • Close to historical and cultural sites in the surrounding region
  • Approximately 1.5 hours from Çukurova International Airport (COV), ensuring convenient international access
