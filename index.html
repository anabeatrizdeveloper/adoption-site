Adoption & Travel Options

The Rescue Retreat

To make international adoption more flexible, we offer two options depending on your schedule, comfort, and budget.

A Vacation with a Purpose

Meet your new companion where the Mediterranean meets history.

When you adopt internationally from KİMCANKO Silifke, you may travel to Silifke, meet your pet in person, and stay as our guest for up to one week while you bond before traveling home together.

Spend your days exploring the coast, relaxing by the sea, and getting to know your new best friend with support from our team.

Inquire About the Rescue Retreat
Option A

The Rescue Retreat (Pick-up)

Travel to Silifke to meet and collect your pet in person.

What’s included

  • Complimentary accommodation at our seaside guest house for up to 7 days
  • Guidance and support throughout your stay
  • Assistance with airport pickup and drop-off
  • Full preparation of your pet for international travel

What’s not included

  • Airfare
  • Meals
  • Personal or local expenses
The benefit: No transport fees, time to bond with your pet, and a unique travel experience on the Mediterranean coast.
Option B

Home Delivery (Courier / Flight Volunteer)

We arrange for your pet to be transported to your nearest major airport.

How it works

  • Via a flight volunteer
  • Or through a professional animal courier

Cost

  • Adopter covers the flight volunteer’s fee or courier service
  • Plus standard adoption preparation costs

What’s Included

  • Private guest room for up to 7 days
  • Guidance and support throughout your stay
  • Help with airport pickup / drop-off
  • Full preparation of your pet for international travel

Adoption Costs & Logistics

The adoption fee strictly covers your pet’s preparation and export process.

  • Health & Safety: Rabies titre testing, vaccinations, microchipping
  • Travel Gear: IATA-approved travel crate
  • Documentation: Official government export certificates

How It Works

  • Where is it located? Silifke, approximately 1.5 hours from the nearest major airport.
  • Do you help with transport? Yes, we assist with airport pickup and drop-off.
  • What about travel documents? We handle the required veterinary certifications and paperwork.

Local Experience

Make the most of your trip

Your stay can be more than a pickup. It can also be a meaningful visit to one of the most beautiful coastal areas in Türkiye.

  • Visit Taşucu’s beaches
  • Enjoy breakfast in Narlıkuyu
  • Explore the ancient ruins of Olba
A meaningful journey for you and your pet
