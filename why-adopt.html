Animal Care & Medical Standards

Every animal in our care receives consistent veterinary attention and is prepared according to international health requirements before adoption.

Sterilized & Rabies Vaccinated All adult animals are sterilized and vaccinated prior to adoption.
Routine Parasite Prevention Internal and external treatments are maintained regularly.
Ongoing Veterinary Supervision Health is monitored throughout the entire care and preparation period.
Trusted Veterinary Partners All medical care is carried out by our trusted local veterinary partners, based on the individual needs of each animal.

Adoption Process & International Support

We work in coordination with trusted partner organizations to support responsible international adoptions, ensuring a structured and transparent process.

Partner-Based Adoption Adoptions are carried out in collaboration with established NGOs in the destination country.
Preparation & Compliance We ensure all required medical procedures and documentation are completed prior to travel.
3-Month Timeline Due to international titre regulations, a mandatory 3 month waiting period applies. We care for and prepare your pet throughout this time.

Adoption & Travel Options

To make the adoption process more flexible, we offer two options depending on your schedule, location, and budget.

Option A: The Rescue Retreat (Pick-up)

What it is: Travel to Silifke to meet and collect your pet in person.

What’s included: Complimentary accommodation at our seaside guest house for up to 7 days.

What’s not included: Airfare, meals, and personal/local expenses.

  • No transport fees, since you act as the flight volunteer
  • Time to bond with your pet before traveling home
  • A unique experience on the Mediterranean coast

Option B: Home Delivery (Courier / Flight Volunteer)

What it is: We arrange for your pet to be transported to your nearest major airport.

How it works: Either via a flight volunteer (pet in cabin) or through a professional animal courier.

Cost: The adopter covers the flight volunteer’s fee or courier service, plus standard adoption preparation costs.

The Rescue Retreat

A vacation with a purpose. Why not meet your new best friend where the Mediterranean meets history?

When you adopt internationally from KİMCANKO Silifke, we invite you to stay as our guest. Enjoy up to one week in our seaside home, free of charge, while you bond with your pet before traveling home together.

Spend your days exploring the pristine beaches of Taşucu, visiting ancient sites along the Turquoise Coast, or simply relaxing on the terrace with your new companion.

We handle all veterinary certifications and assist with airport transport to Mersin (COV), making the journey as smooth as possible.

What’s Included

  • Private guest room for up to 7 days
  • Guidance and support throughout your stay
  • Assistance with airport pickup and drop-off
  • Full preparation of your pet for international travel

Adoption Costs & Logistics

We ask for an adoption fee that strictly covers expenses for your pet’s preparation:

  • Rabies titre testing
  • Vaccinations and microchipping
  • IATA-approved travel crate
  • Official government export certificates

How It Works

Where is it located?

Silifke, approximately 1.5 hours from Mersin (COV) Airport.

Do you help with transport?

Yes, we assist with airport pickup and drop-off.

What about travel documents?

We handle all required international veterinary certifications and paperwork.

What can I enjoy during my trip?

  • Visit Taşucu’s beaches
  • Enjoy breakfast in Narlıkuyu
  • Explore the ancient ruins of Olba
