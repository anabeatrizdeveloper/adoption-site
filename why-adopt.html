Why Adopt With KİMCANKO?

Trusted rescue and international adoption, built on experience and transparency.

At KİMCANKO Silifke, we combine hands-on rescue work, long-term animal care, and responsible international adoption support. Our goal is not only to help animals find homes, but to guide adopters through the process with honesty, clarity, and care.

Travel-Ready Medical Protocol

Every animal is carefully prepared to meet international health and travel requirements before adoption.

All medical care is carried out by our trusted local veterinary partners, under continued supervision and according to the needs of each animal.

Sterilized & Rabies Vaccinated Core procedures completed prior to international adoption.
Routine Parasite Prevention Internal and external parasite protocols are maintained.
Ongoing Veterinary Supervision Health is monitored throughout the preparation period.

International Adoption Support

We work in coordination with trusted NGOs to facilitate safe and responsible international adoptions.

Partner-Based Process Adoptions are carried out in collaboration with established organizations in the destination country.
Preparation & Compliance We ensure all required medical procedures and documentation are completed prior to travel.
4-Month Timeline Due to international titre regulations, a mandatory 1+3 month waiting period applies. We care for and prepare your pet throughout this entire time.

We remain involved and available throughout the process, from initial inquiry to your pet’s arrival.
