The Rescue Retreat

Travel to Silifke, meet your pet in person, and return home together. This option offers a more personal adoption experience and gives you time to bond before travel.

Inquire About This Option

What it is

You travel to Silifke to meet and collect your pet in person, then travel home together.

What’s included

  • Complimentary accommodation for up to 7 days
  • Guidance and support throughout your stay
  • Assistance with airport pickup and drop-off
  • Full preparation of your pet for international travel

What’s not included

  • Airfare
  • Meals
  • Personal or local expenses

Adoption Costs & Logistics

  • Rabies titre testing
  • Vaccinations and microchipping
  • IATA-approved travel crate
  • Official export documentation

Location & Support

  • Silifke, approximately 1.5 hours from the nearest major airport
  • Airport pickup and drop-off assistance available
  • Support throughout the preparation process

Why choose this option?

It removes transport fees, gives you time to connect with your pet, and creates a more meaningful and personal adoption journey.

Local Experience

  • Visit Taşucu’s beaches
  • Enjoy breakfast in Narlıkuyu
  • Explore the ancient ruins of Olba

Guest Experience

During your stay, you can spend time observing your pet, asking questions, and preparing for the journey home with guidance from our team.

Who it is ideal for

Adopters who want a more hands-on and emotional adoption experience, and who are able to travel to meet their pet in person.
